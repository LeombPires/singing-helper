# Singing helper

A one-octave piano to find a note and hold it while singing.

- Tap a key to hear the note. Notes ring on for several seconds, like with the sustain pedal down.
- **−** and **+** move the scale down or up a semitone (C2 major to C6 major). The keyboard always shows the scale from its root to the root above, with its notes marked by a red dot.
- **Autoplay** plays the major scale shown (C major to start: C D E F G A B C) up and back down, 5 seconds per note, until stopped. Tapping a key while it plays continues from that note.
- On a computer the keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play the notes too, and Z / X move the scale down / up.

Works offline once opened, and can be installed on a phone:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → "Install app".

To update: replace `index.html` and bump the version in `sw.js` (`singing-helper-v1` → `v2`).
